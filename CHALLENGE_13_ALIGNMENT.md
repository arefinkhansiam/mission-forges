## 3. Why Mission Forge is a Winning Project
1. **No "Toy" Physics:** Many space games make rocket thrust feel like arcade cars. Mission Forge shows students that every extra kilogram of payload demands more propellant or higher engine specific impulse ($I_{sp}$).
2. **Empowering Young Learners:** With the Ayesha Voice AI, kids don't need a degree in astrophysics to understand why their spacecraft lost signal at Neptune or ran out of battery at Jupiter.
3. **Rock-Solid Reliability:** The application will never crash in front of judges or classroom students due to comprehensive prototype resilience.
